# Phase 17 Plan 02: Establish dependency-safe app, auth, store, and layout ownership

<objective>
Remove shared-to-feature and feature-to-app reversals at the composition boundary, and move MainLayout to its app owner without changing session, permission, navigation, preload, or DOM behavior.
</objective>
