## 1. Problem Statement & Root-Cause Matrix (`M1–M10`)

Following the completion of rounds `E`, `F`, `G`, `H`, `I`, `J`, `K`, and `L`, a line-by-line audit across all 18 MRX route/view leaves and Backend services identified 10 remaining causes of route/chunk transition flash, scope-switch phase mismatch, search-empty table teardown, sub-tab skeleton flicker, seam box-model shifts, cross-tab cache misses, and over-eager SQL entity loading:

| ID | Category | Owner Files | Root Cause | Exact Remedy (`AC-M*`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **`M1`** | Must fix | `frontend/src/App.tsx` | `AdvancedSettingsPage` (`/settings/advanced`, one of the 18 MRX route leaves) and `ApprovalRulesPage` are still wrapped in `React.lazy(...)` while the other 6 MRX station pages were promoted to eager imports in `G8`. Navigating to `/settings/advanced` in MRX mode suspends `<Outlet />` inside `<AppShell>` and flashes `<WorkbenchSkeleton>` before mounting the page. | **`AC-M1`**: Promote `AdvancedSettingsPage` and `ApprovalRulesPage` to eager static imports in `App.tsx` so all MRX-eligible routes mount in 0ms without a lazy-chunk `<Suspense>` skeleton flash, while preserving `ProtectedRoute`'s authoritative `DOM-MODE-1` bootstrap contract. |

## 2. Invariants & Non-Goals

- **Strict Mode Isolation (`DOM-MODE-1`)**: Zero behavioral regressions in `DEFAULT` mode; `ProtectedRoute` continues to block stored `DEFAULT` routes until `systemApi.getOperationalMode()` resolves.
- **Single Styling Authority**: All classes use existing Tailwind / `index.css` tokens (`h-9`, `min-w-[155px]`, `opacity-70`, `delay-150`, etc.).
- **Zero Tautological Tests (`TA-1..TA-5`)**: All tests exercise real components, hooks, and EF Core services with backend-grounded fixtures.
